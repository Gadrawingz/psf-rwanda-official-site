# PSF Members Survey

## Registration Guide

This guide will walk you through the process of logging in, registering new members, and viewing existing members on the PSF website.
Getting Started: Login and Registration

If you are a new user, follow these steps to access the PSF portal:
1. **Navigate to the Official PSF Website:** Open your web browser and go to https://psf.org.rw/.
2. **Access the Login Page**: Click on the "Login" option, typically found under the navigation section. You can also directly access the login page here: https://psf.org.rw/panel/login.
3. **Enter Your Credentials**: When the login form appears:
   - **Role**: In the first field, select "staff" (for DM).
   - **Email Address**: In the second field, enter your official office email address.
   - **Password**: In the third field, enter the correct password, which by default is: "@PSF2025p".
4. **Log In**: Click the "Login" button. 

## Post-Login: Membership Activities

Upon successful login, you will be redirected to the homepage. For activities related to membership surveys and registration, use the dropdown menu under "PSF Members":

------
A. PSF Members
- **New Member**: This link will help you start the registration of new companies and their representatives.
- **View Members**: This link will show you all registered members.
> NB: You'll be able to see only the members you have registered.
